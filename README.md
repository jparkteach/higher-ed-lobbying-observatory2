# Higher Education Lobbying Observatory · 2004–2024

[Open the dashboard](https://jparkteach.github.io/higher-ed-lobbying-observatory2/)

Explore 45,756 campus-year records by year, sector and location. Lobbying outcomes are available through 2022. The 2023 and 2024 outcomes are pending.

In the annual spending chart, public-campus totals for 2015–2022 are multiplied by 1.49 as a scenario assumption. Expand **Annual chart data** to compare recorded and displayed amounts.

[Camp’s models](https://jparkteach.github.io/higher-ed-lobbying-observatory2/#models) compares six verified 2004–2014 regressions with their 2015–2022 counterparts. Choose an outcome and sample to see estimates, confidence intervals and sample sizes side by side. The tab also includes sector-specific hypothesis tests, matched eight-year comparisons and downloadable results. Spending regressions use recorded spending in 2014 dollars.

To use the dashboard offline, download this repository, extract it and open `index.html` in a browser.
