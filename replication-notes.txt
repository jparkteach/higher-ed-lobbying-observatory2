Camp’s final grouped file reproduces the six principal regressions in dissertation Tables 20 and 21. All 76 estimated coefficients or odds ratios, all 76 standard errors, every significance marker, the six sample sizes and the six fit statistics agree at published precision. The sample consists of 1,956 original analysis units (1,826 standalones and 130 systems). There are 721 ever-registrants (644 standalones and 77 systems). WHAT THE RESULTS MEAN
Across all units and among standalones, higher federal R&D dependence is associated with higher registration odds and spending, after adjustment. Higher Pell dependence is associated with lower registration odds, with no statistically significant adjusted spending association. In the systems-only regressions neither funding-dependence measure is statistically significant. For the sector-specific hypotheses, nonprofit R&D dependence correlates with spending (r = 0.53, p < 0.001). For-profit Pell dependence has a weak, statistically insignificant correlation with spending (r = 0.08, p = 0.306).

Camp entered both budget shares as fractions. In the all-units model, a ten-percentage-point increase in R&D share implies 69.2% higher registration odds and $24,263 higher average spending. The same change in Pell share implies 26.9% lower odds. These are adjusted associations. The dissertation’s prose gives a different scaling. Its $242,629 R&D coefficient corresponds to a change of 1.0 in the budget fraction, so the estimate per percentage point is $2,426.

WHAT THE CROSSWALK COVERS
combined_reviewed_crosswalk.csv combines all 16 reviewed cases. It contains 1,430 unique campus-year keys representing 70 campus identifiers. The two UNC reviews overlap and their common campus-years appear once with both review numbers. Of the 1,430 entries, 1,188 link to a study-panel row. The others preserve the review’s coverage, including campuses absent from the study that year. The reviewed group memberships can cover only the campuses in scope for that review.

Join on unit_id and year. Use panel_row_present to select actual study rows. corrected_group records the resolved institutional assignment for the later harmonized analysis. raw_2004_2014_group and raw_2015_2024_group retain the respective source labels. reconstructed_camp_group identifies the original final analysis unit where its campus membership can be reconstructed. camp_assignment_status distinguishes excluded, absent, context-only and post-2014 rows. A blank historical group is never evidence of an independent institution. Reviewed exclusions are affirmative findings only. The evidence file column points to the original detailed crosswalks within the project and ZIP archive.

CAMPUS-LEVEL VERIFICATION
333 historical campus-year assignments reconstruct 27 original analysis units, including all 16 flagged IDs and 11 associated IDs. Average within each group-year, then average the available years with equal weight. For all 27 units the following seven fields agree with the archived group totals or means within numerical storage precision: undergraduate FTE, budget, Pell, federal R&D receipts, registration, hospital and medical program. For example, final group 81 includes both Anderson institutions even in 2004. Group 270 combines the Indiana, Kansas and Tennessee Bethels in 2004–2009 and adds the Virginia college in 2010–2014.

The separate dollar check uses Camp’s documented annual multipliers on the 2019 file’s client amount (crpamt), with single-precision storage for the generated values and group means. The available evidence does not establish the full intervening dollar edits. The regression comparison uses the archived spending values for both groups.
