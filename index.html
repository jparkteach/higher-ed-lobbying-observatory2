Sources & methods

Reading the data

Campus records

Each row identifies a campus and year. Registration includes filings with zero spending. Charts use observed outcomes and show gaps where values are missing. Membership and reporting change across the two sources in 2015.

Comparable dollars

Campus spending uses 2014 dollars. Earlier records retain the supplied adjusted amount. Later amounts use inflation-adjusted annual dollars, with zero assigned to blank spending only for explicit nonregistrants. Allocations and possible campus/system overlap affect spending sums.

Institution groupings

The 16 reviewed groupings are available in campus details. Camp’s 1,956 original analysis units underpin the models. The combined campus panel supports descriptive comparisons through the years with observed outcomes.
